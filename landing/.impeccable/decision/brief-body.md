## Direction contract

THESIS: A hallmark certifies that metal meets a legal fineness without saying which mine it came from; this page is a struck plate whose row of five marks is the product story (supplier mark sealed · fineness ≥ 16 % · assayed on the manufacturer's device · lots counted once · Compliant struck on chain). It refuses both the deep-green SaaS scroll of screenshot cards and the cream editorial sheet of hairlines and tracked mono labels.

OWN-WORLD: Brushed light steel (#D9DDE3 family) as the continuous plate, one full-bleed matte brass nameplate (#A9842F, highlights #C9A24D, shadow #7A5A14) for the marks, one oxidised-steel dark passage (#1B1E23) for the bench. Everything important is struck: debossed cartouches (octagon for fineness numerals, oval for the sponsor, rounded rectangle for the date letter, circle for the control mark carrying the brand's measure-and-strickle glyph), engraved text with a light edge below and a dark edge above, hairline seams where plate meets plate. Bitter 800 for every heading and every struck numeral, IBM Plex Sans for reading, mono only for hashes. No eyebrows, no cards, no icon tiles, no glass. Amber lamp (#E0A100) only on the delayed clock. Green only on Compliant.

STORY: The visitor recognises the oldest conformity mark in Europe, reads the five marks as "this meets the standard and nobody sees the source", sees the law that demands both, watches one object pass through four assay states, sees the three real screens on the bench, reads what is struck in public and what stays in the sponsor's book, reads the three disclaimers, and takes the assay certificate (the memo). Judge finds the code links under the certificate.

Centre: a row of five struck cartouches ~200 px each, struck one by one on scroll (scale 1.06→1, deboss depth 0→1, 240 ms, 120 ms stagger), each with its meaning set beneath in the slab: Supplier · sealed / ≥ 16 % · recycled cobalt / Assayed on your device / Lot 8 of 8 · counted once / Compliant · Reg.
